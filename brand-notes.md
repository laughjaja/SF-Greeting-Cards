# Brand Notes

## Direction
The greeting email should align with the Sarah Fell site tone:
- clean
- restrained
- premium corporate
- industrial-professional
- structured, not decorative

## Palette behavior
- keep the email shell neutral
- use light backgrounds and charcoal text
- avoid imposing a beige or gold wrapper palette
- let the hero asset carry the emotional tone

## Envelope concept
- closed envelope on initial frame
- automatic open motion
- message revealed inside the letter
- final state should feel open and centered

## Production notes
- real email first, not a landing page
- no JavaScript
- safe email wrapper
- animated GIF hero plus static fallback image
